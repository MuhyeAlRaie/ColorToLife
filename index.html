Color the five zones

  • 1Body - the big head and round tummy area
  • 2Belly - the oval tummy patch
  • 3Spikes - the five back plates
  • 4Feet - both little feet
  • 5Arms - both small arms

Coloring tips

  • Press a little harder so colors are bright and bold.
  • Stay inside the thick black lines.
  • Leave the eyes and mouth black and white.
  • Each zone can have a different color - be creative!

For grown-ups: print at 100% scale on plain white A4 paper. When scanning, good light and a flat page give the best color match.
